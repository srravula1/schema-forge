## Section 1 — Inbound channels and signal patterns

**What are the primary channels through which qualified leads arrive?**

> 1. SAP-field co-sell (SAP AE or Regional VP referral): ~55% of pipeline by volume.
>    Signal pattern: SAP AE emails intro saying "I have an account that needs a Joule implementation partner." The AE has internal co-sell quota motivation. Quality varies: ~22% of SAP AE referrals become active pipeline after end-customer budget confirmation.
>
> 2. Big4 / SI-partner pull-through: ~30% of pipeline by volume.
>    Signal pattern: Deloitte, Accenture, Capgemini, or NTT Data SAP practice brings us in as a named subcontractor on a signed prime contract. These are the highest-quality leads: end-customer is already contracted, budget is confirmed by the prime, and our scope is pre-defined.
>
> 3. CIO/CFO warm referral from past clients: ~10% of pipeline.
>    Signal pattern: Direct inbound email or LinkedIn message from a CIO who was referred by a past pilot client. No SAP-field involvement. Fastest sales cycle.
>
> 4. SAP conference / SAPPHIRE / TechEd inbound: ~5% of pipeline.
>    Signal pattern: Business card exchange at a SAP event followed by inbound email. Variable quality.

**What signals most reliably separate a real buyer from a tire-kicker at the channel level?**

> For SAP-field co-sell: the AE has already had a budget conversation with the end-customer CIO or CFO before the intro call — i.e., the co-sell is not a fishing expedition. Real indicator: end-customer has a Joule pilot line item in their current or next-quarter IT budget.
> For SI-partner: the prime contract is signed and we have a named SOW slot. Everything before SOW signature is channel noise.
> For direct inbound: the contact mentions a specific use case and a specific timeline in the first message. "We want to deploy Joule for FI month-end before our fiscal year close in Q3" is a buyer. "We're exploring AI options in SAP" is a 6-18 month nurture.

## Section 2 — SAP-field co-sell motion

**How do SAP Account Executives and Regional VPs typically engage with you?**

> SAP AEs contact us via LinkedIn DM or email with a brief intro: "I have an account running S/4HANA on-prem that wants to activate Joule — they have budget but need a partner. Can we set up a three-way call?" We agree to the intro call. After the intro call, we do our own discovery with the end-customer to confirm budget and use case before accepting the co-sell in CRM.

**What is the failure mode you see most in SAP-field co-sell?**

> SAP AEs refer accounts where the end-customer is interested in Joule "in theory" but has no allocated budget and no concrete project. The AE has quota incentive to nominate co-sell partners regardless of account readiness. We have had 3 deals this year where we spent 4-6 weeks in discovery only to find the end-customer budget was not real. The SAP AE was not lying — they genuinely believed the account was ready — but the end-customer's internal budget process hadn't allocated anything.

**What are the signals that a SAP AE co-sell is high-quality vs. channel noise?**

> High-quality: AE has a named budget line item confirmed with the CIO/CFO, a specific use case (not just "Joule"), and a defined timeline (pilot to start in the next 60-90 days).
> Channel noise: AE refers "an interested account" without confirming budget or use case. The intro call is exploratory from the AE's side, not the end-customer's.

## Section 3 — Big4 / SI-partner pull-through motion

**How do Big4 and large IT consulting partners engage with you?**

> Typically a Managing Director or Partner in their SAP practice reaches out because their transformation program includes a Joule scope they are not staffed for. They do not want to build a Joule practice from scratch; they want a specialist subcontractor. The conversation is commercial (what is your day rate, what is your capacity, what is your insurance/E&O coverage?) before it is technical.

**What makes a Big4 pull-through a real opportunity vs. a false positive?**

> The prime contract must be signed or in final negotiation. A Big4 MD who is "exploring subcontractors for a proposal" is not a real opportunity — they may never win the prime, and even if they win, they may staff it internally. We track Big4 relationships as "pipeline potential" only once there is a signed prime contract and a named SOW slot for our work.

**What is the failure mode you see most in SI-partner pull-through?**

> Big4 MD relationship without a live program: we have been cultivated by two separate SI partners for months, attending their partner events, doing reference calls, and being presented in proposals — then the prime contract was either lost to another SI or delayed 12 months. The relationship is real; the deal is not. We now require a signed prime contract or final-stage LOI before investing delivery capacity in an SI-partner pursuit.

## Section 4 — Inbound direct

**How do direct inbound referrals arrive, and what is the quality pattern?**

> CIO-to-CIO referrals (a past pilot client refers their peer) are the highest quality and shortest cycle. The referring CIO has implicitly pre-qualified the prospect ("they run SAP, they have a Joule question, I told them to call you"). The first call is already past the "what do you do" stage.
> SAPPHIRE/TechEd inbounds vary: some are real (attendee saw a problem they recognized from the keynote), most are relationship-building with no near-term project.

## Section 5 — Warm-prospect definition

**When do you consider a prospect "warm" and ready for active qualification?**

> For SAP-field co-sell: when the end-customer CIO or CFO has confirmed (directly with us, not through the AE) that a Joule pilot budget exists or is in active procurement. Without this, the account stays in co-sell nurture regardless of AE enthusiasm.
> For SI-partner: when the prime contract is signed and we have a named SOW slot.
> For direct inbound: when the contact has named a specific use case and a timeline within 90 days.
> For conference inbound: when a follow-up email from the prospect references a specific S/4HANA or Joule challenge within 2 weeks of the event. After 2 weeks without follow-up, move to 6-month nurture.
