# Template 02 — ICP Interview

**Mode:** Live call
**Duration:** 60 minutes
**Attendees:** Founder + Head of Sales (both required; do not run with only one)

---

## FDE pre-call checklist

- [ ] Template 01 (company overview) received and read
- [ ] Calendar invite sent with this agenda attached
- [ ] Recording consent obtained in advance or confirmed at call start
- [ ] Blank section below ready to fill in during or immediately after the call

**FDE note:** This is the most important session of the engagement. Budget the full 60 minutes. If you can only get 45, cut Section 4 (the triage exercise) — do not cut Sections 1–3. The closed-won / closed-lost deal walk-through is the single highest-signal input for ICP overrides. Do not shortcut it.

---

## Agenda (share with attendees beforehand)

| Time | Topic |
|---|---|
| 0–5 min | Framing: what we're doing and why it matters |
| 5–25 min | Last 5 closed-won deals |
| 25–40 min | Last 5 closed-lost deals (qualified prospect, you lost) |
| 40–50 min | Deals you regret taking |
| 50–58 min | Triage exercise |
| 58–60 min | The outsider question |

---

## Opening script (read verbatim or close to it)

> "We are going to spend the next hour going through real deals, not describing your ICP in the abstract. We have found that asking 'who is your ideal customer?' produces marketing copy. Looking at specific deals produces the signal we actually need. Everything you tell us stays inside this engagement — it goes into the schema, not into any report or external document. The more specific and honest you can be, including about deals that went badly, the better the output. Ready?"

## Section 3 — Deals you regret taking

**FDE prompt:** "Tell me about three deals you wish you had not taken. Not prospects you declined — clients you actually took on, and post-sale things went wrong. What went wrong, and what was the signal you missed or ignored before you signed?"

**FDE note:** This section produces the disqualification rules and the failure-mode additions. Push for specifics on the missed pre-sale signal — "they seemed rushed" is not enough; "they couldn't name a single internal stakeholder who would own the project" is a usable signal.

## Section 4 — Triage exercise

**FDE prompt:** "Imagine I gave you a list of 1,000 companies — public companies, startups, nonprofits, everything. You have to triage them into three buckets: 'worth a call this week,' 'nurture for 6 months,' and 'never.' Walk me through exactly how you'd think through that triage. What signals or attributes do you look for to move something into each bucket?"

**FDE note:** Let them think out loud. Do not suggest categories — capture their natural language. This maps directly to the priority-score modifiers in the schema.
